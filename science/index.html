01 / Learn the big three

Break it. Move it. Drop it.

Sediment means loose material such as small rock pieces, sand and soil. Think of it as the tiny travelers in our story.

02 / Follow a rock's journey

One rock. Three changes.

Tap each step to follow what happens.

Detective tip: Water can do all three!
Water freezes in a crack and breaks rock: weathering.
Water carries the pieces away: erosion.
Water slows and the pieces settle: deposition.
Two landforms to know

Delta: land built from sediment at a river's mouth, where it enters a lake or ocean.

Sand dune: a pile of sand built up by wind. Wind moving sand is erosion. Sand collecting into a dune is deposition.
