FOR GROWN-UPS

Little learner settings

Sky: tall letters. Plane: small-letter height. Grass: where letters sit. Worm: where tails go. Left-handed mode moves the labels and controls; it never mirrors letters.

Letters use simple print handwriting. Watch the demonstration, then trace each stroke in order. The next stroke starts after lifting a finger. Little letters use a single-storey a and g.

Sounds start after a tap. Spoken prompts depend on voices available on the device. Animal effects are disabled. Completion uses a short musical reward. Optional quiet music pauses during speech and tracing. No accounts, advertising, third-party analytics, or uploaded child data. Progress stays in this browser and may be lost if browser storage is cleared.

Progress & gentle review

Practice together

Start with the wide trail, watch a stroke, then try it. Pencil mode shows the actual finger or stylus line on a fine guide. Dots reduce the visual support. Tap the dice for shuffled letters; tap any alphabet card to return to choosing letters. A finger on a screen is different from a pencil: try the same letter on paper together too.
