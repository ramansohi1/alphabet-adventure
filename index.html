FOR GROWN-UPS

Little learner settings

Sky: tall letters. Plane: small-letter height. Grass: where letters sit. Worm: where tails go. Left-handed mode moves the labels and controls; it never mirrors letters.

Letters use simple print handwriting. Watch the demonstration, then trace each stroke in order. The next stroke starts after lifting a finger. Little letters use a single-storey a and g.

Sounds start after a tap. Spoken prompts depend on voices available on the device. Animal calls are playful synthesized imitations. Quiet animals, such as rabbits, snails, turtles and jellyfish, have movement or habitat sound effects instead. No accounts, advertising, analytics, or uploaded child data. Progress stays in this browser and may be lost if browser storage is cleared.

Practice together

Start with the wide trail, watch a stroke, then try it. The dots option keeps the starting point but removes the wide track. A finger on a screen is different from a pencil: try the same letter on paper together too.
